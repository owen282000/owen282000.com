Privacy on this website

This notice covers the website owen282000.com. The apps have their own privacy policies: Life Dashboard Companion for Android and for iPhone.

What this site does not do

It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, shows no ads and loads nothing from other websites: no fonts, scripts or images from anyone else. It has no forms and no accounts.

What happens when you visit

The site is served through Cloudflare, which receives your IP address and the page you ask for, as any network in between does. Cloudflare protects the site against attacks and keeps a copy of the pages close to you; it processes that data under its own privacy policy. The web server behind it sees the same request coming from Cloudflare. Neither is used to track you, and no visitor profile is built.
